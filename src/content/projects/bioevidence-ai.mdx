## Overview
BioEvidence turns biomedical questions into evidence-linked answers while keeping model inference local and the retrieval trail visible.

## System design
- Pyserini/Lucene retrieval over a 14.9M-record PubMed index
- Adaptive reranking and evidence selection
- Local LLM generation with on-device inference
- Live integrations with PubMed/NCBI, Europe PMC, and ClinicalTrials.gov

## What was built
- Real-time query-to-citation pipeline
- User-facing Next.js application for answer and evidence inspection
- Local-first inference path designed for privacy and control

## Result
A research system translated into a practical AI application with inspectable evidence and direct links to biomedical sources.
