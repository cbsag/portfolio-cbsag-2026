## Overview
Detect negation cues and their scope in biomedical text with models that can handle imbalanced labels and structured output behavior.

## System design
- Fine-tuned BERT, BioBERT, and ClinicalBERT for cue and scope tagging
- Compared CRF and hierarchical variants under severe class imbalance
- Trained PPO-based sequence-labeling policies using rewards derived from human-annotated spans
- Compared aggregate and label-level results to understand minority-class behavior

## Results
- Established strong supervised baselines for negation cue and scope detection
- Compared PPO-based sequence labeling with supervised approaches
- Produced detailed evidence on model behavior under severe label imbalance
